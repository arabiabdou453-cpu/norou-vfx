# Norouvfx — Creative Video Portfolio

**Norouvfx** is a premium portfolio website for a video editor, filmmaker, VFX artist, and voice-over artist. It presents selected work, creative services, and a direct contact path for clients looking for distinctive visual storytelling.

## Live website

Visit the portfolio: **[norouvfx.framer.website](https://norouvfx.framer.website/)**

## About Norouvfx

Norouvfx helps brands and creators turn ideas into memorable visual experiences. The work combines cinematic editing, motion, sound, visual effects, and a clear understanding of each client’s style and goals.

## Services

- Pre-production planning and creative development
- Video editing and post-production
- Commercial and branded video production
- Social media content and advertising edits
- Motion graphics and visual effects
- Voice-over and visual storytelling

## Portfolio experience

- Curated showreel and selected projects
- Smooth section navigation across the full page
- Responsive experience for desktop and mobile
- Inline YouTube playback with native controls
- Full-screen video support
- Accessible keyboard and touch interactions
- Direct links to Work, Services, About, and Contact
- WhatsApp and Instagram contact options

## Contact

For project inquiries and collaborations, use the contact options available on the [official website](https://norouvfx.framer.website/).
